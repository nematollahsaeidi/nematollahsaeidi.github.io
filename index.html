Nematollah (Emad) Saeidi

AI/ML Engineer and Researcher · MIAAI Group, Danube Private University, Austria

Medical Image Analysis & Artificial Intelligence (MIAAI) Group

Danube Private University

Wiener Neustadt, Austria

I am an AI professional and researcher with over 7 years of experience in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and medical image analysis. I work as an AI Researcher in the Medical Image Analysis & Artificial Intelligence (MIAAI) group at Danube Private University (DPU), Austria, where I contribute to projects applying machine learning and computer vision to medical images. I completed my PhD in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Isfahan, Iran, in October 2026, with a thesis on content-based medical image retrieval using graph neural networks.

I have developed more than 10 AI services, including an image search engine, a recommendation system, an attendance register system, an object detection system, a drawing robot, and customer behavior analysis. Before DPU, I spent five years at FANAP Co., where I built AI-driven solutions in deep learning, computer vision, and marketing data science, and also provided RFPs and business plans within the AI open innovation ecosystem. Today I also work as an AI engineer at Hyrcan Technology Development Co., building local RAG-based voice assistants, speaker recognition, and face recognition systems.

My expertise includes retrieval, classification, clustering, and image analysis, with advanced techniques such as graph neural networks, foundation models, adversarial variational graph autoencoders, transfer learning, and generative AI. I work mainly with Python, PyTorch, and TensorFlow, and I have published in reputable journals and conferences. I serve as a reviewer for several journals, including Applied Soft Computing, Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine, and Biomedical Signal Processing and Control.

I was awarded the Ernst Mach Grant of the Austrian Agency for International Cooperation in Education and Research (OeAD) in 2023 and 2024 for research stays at MIAAI, Danube Private University.

Feel free to browse my publications, projects, CV, and code on GitHub.
