A solution for recommendation and customer analysis. It includes a sequence-to-sequence deep learning model for ranking, association rule-based, hybrid, content-based and collaborative filtering systems, customer clustering, and feature engineering for candidate generation. It also covers data collection, table treatment, data cleansing, and database management.

Associated with FANAP.
