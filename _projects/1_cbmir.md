This project explores the use of recently developed vision and vision-language foundation models for content-based medical image retrieval (CBMIR) across both 2D and 3D medical images. It also investigates the impact of image size on retrieval performance.

Associated with Danube Private University Austria (DPU). Code: [Medical-Image-Retrieval](https://github.com/nematollahsaeidi/Medical-Image-Retrieval).
