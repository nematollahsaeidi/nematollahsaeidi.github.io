I'm an AI researcher and engineer (PhD in Artificial Intelligence) with 8 years of experience across medical image analysis, computer vision, information retrieval, and applied AI systems.  

Since April 2026, I have served as the sole AI specialist at a startup, with end-to-end responsibility for the company's AI services. My work focuses on multilingual information retrieval and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over enterprise data, and on intelligent document processing combining OCR, vision-language models, and LLM-based structured extraction. Before that, I built local RAG voice assistants, speaker recognition, and face recognition systems at Hyrcan (November 2025 – March 2026). Before that, from September 2023 to September 2025, I was an AI Researcher in the MIAAI group at Danube Private University (Austria), working on content-based medical image retrieval and histopathology image classification using graph neural networks and foundation models. Before that, over five years at FANAP (2018–2023), I delivered 10+ AI services across visual search, object detection, recommendation, customer analytics, and medical image search.
