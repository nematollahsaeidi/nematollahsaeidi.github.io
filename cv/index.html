Professional Summary

AI engineer and researcher (PhD in Artificial Intelligence) with 8 years of experience across applied machine learning, computer vision, generative AI, information retrieval, and medical image analysis. Currently the sole AI specialist at a startup, responsible for multilingual hybrid search and RAG over enterprise data, and for intelligent document processing with OCR, vision-language models, and LLM-based structured extraction. Research focuses on graph neural networks and foundation models for medical image retrieval and computational pathology, published in Q1/Q2 journals.
